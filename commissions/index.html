Commissions

Sharon works to a personalised brief. Every commissioned piece begins with a conversation — about what matters to you, what the work is for, and what story it should tell. Whether you have a clear vision or just a starting point, she'll help shape it into something made entirely for you. She takes on a small number of commissions at a time, so enquire early.

What to expect

01

Share your brief

Fill in the form with your idea. The more detail the better — references, sizes, occasions, and anything that matters to you.

02

Initial conversation

Sharon will get back to you to talk through your vision, timeline, and pricing. Nothing is agreed until you're happy.

03

Creation

Once agreed, Sharon gets to work. You'll receive updates as the piece develops — it's a collaboration, not a transaction.

04

Delivery

Your finished piece is carefully packaged and delivered to you.
