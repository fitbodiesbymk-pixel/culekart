Commissions

Sharon's commissioned pieces are deeply personal. She works with clients to embed meaningful objects, colours, and memories into the work — a dialogue is always at the heart of the process. Whether you're after a bespoke alphabet piece, a travel painting, or something entirely your own, get in touch and she'll be happy to discuss what's possible.
